Local Filter

Censor what you'd rather not read.

Features

Add topics you want to avoid, and redeeming topics that save them. Every (sentence, topic) pair is sent to Jev's noul API, and a weighted sum decides.

In short: focus on what you want to see more of.

Why Jev

The initial idea was to use a 100% local model (such as Xenova/DistilBERT) — but that was too slow and clunky to set up in an extension.

After Jev's launch I thought this was a perfect fit: a good, general-purpose classifier that can zero-shot topics.

What's next

I am looking at open, local alternatives that are blazing fast — even (especially) on a CPU. Feel free to reach out to talk about that. :)
