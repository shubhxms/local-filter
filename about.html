Local Filter

Censor what you'd rather not read.

What it does

Local Filter decides block by block whether it's about the topics you'd rather avoid, and censors what matches. Blocks carrying your redeeming qualities are spared. 100% local version coming soon.

How it decides

Each block's sentences are scored against your topics in a single pass by Jev — TypeSafe AI's model. A block is censored when enough of its sentences match; it is redeemed when there is enough redeeming quality. Verdicts are cached per page.

Keyboard

⌘⌃B filters the current page. Every control in the popup and this page is reachable by Tab; list entries can be edited by clicking or focusing them and pressing Enter.
