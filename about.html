Local Filter

Censor what you'd rather not read.

What it does

Local Filter reads the page you're on, decides block by block whether it's about the topics you'd rather avoid, and censors what matches. Blocks carrying your redeeming qualities are spared, at the mercy you choose. The only thing that leaves your machine is the text being judged.

How it decides

Sentences are the measurement; blocks are the action. Each block's sentences are scored against your topics in a single pass by Jev — TypeSafe AI's System One model, which answers typed questions with probabilities instead of prose. A block is censored when enough of its sentences match; it is redeemed when its redeeming evidence keeps up with its censoring evidence. Verdicts are cached per page, so re-filtering costs nothing.

Keyboard

⌘⌃B filters the current page. Every control in the popup and this page is reachable by Tab; list entries can be edited by clicking or focusing them and pressing Enter.
